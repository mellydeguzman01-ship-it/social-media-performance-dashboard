Key Insights & Observations

Facebook dominates reach — with 27,124 views (66.3% of total), Facebook is by far the most impactful platform. However, engagement rate remains relatively low at 9.0%, suggesting broad reach but limited interaction.
LinkedIn has the highest engagement rate — at 38.1%, LinkedIn significantly outperforms other platforms, with 1,210 reactions from 3,172 views.
Dec 2025 was the peak performance month — generating 16,175 total views (40% of all views).
Content output varies by platform — Regular posting drives sustained engagement. Monitor platform-specific performance weekly for best results.
Data tracking across all 6 platforms — Daily metrics synced every 2 hours from Lark Base Marketing Calendar. Dashboard auto-refreshed with live data.
Total content published: 232 — across all platforms (Nov 2025 – Oct 2026).

Recommendations

1. Maintain consistent posting schedule — Regular posting drives sustained engagement. Aim for minimum 2x/week per active platform.
2. Double down on LinkedIn strategy — With its outsized engagement rate, LinkedIn posts generate high interaction. Increase LinkedIn content frequency.
3. Ensure daily tracking coverage — Daily metrics must be logged consistently for all 6 platforms to maintain data accuracy in the dashboard.
4. Leverage Facebook reach for conversions — Facebook’s massive viewership represents a broad audience. Add call-to-action posts to convert views into engagement.
